           which after evaluating the first digit hits `C[1]` in Context 2

5. 9-STATE EXTENSIONS OF `C4_1` AND `C4_2` (`backtrack_c4_9state.py`)
================================================================================

We ran an exact lazy-branching backtracking search over all 9-state extensions
of `C4_1` and `C4_2` (states `A..I` with 1 undefined halt slot `---`, totaling
78,336 halting 9-state machines per base machine):
  - `C4_1`: 19,409 lazy execution branches explored; 485 halting 9-state machines
    reach `VALID_ODD_K` on both k = 3 and k = 21 with M-extension (`ds(M+1) = ds(M) + [1]`).
  - `C4_2`: 19,805 lazy execution branches explored; 519 halting 9-state machines
    reach `VALID_ODD_K` on both k = 3 and k = 21 with M-extension.

Why Zero of the 485 + 519 Machines Halt After Full Round 2 (at k_2):
  1. Constant Parity of `2k - 3`:
     In the exhaustion configuration `(10)^{2k-3} 11001 C[1] 1 (0011)^M 001`,
     the number of `10` pairs is `2k - 3`, which is ODD for every integer k.
     Any gadget that chews one `10` pair per cycle (`k -> k - 1`) executes the
     exact same transition sequence at the left end for both odd k and even k.
  2. Transition Budget in 2-Pair Chewers (`k -> k - 2`):
     Gadgets that chew `(10)^4` (two `10` pairs, `k -> k - 2`) per cycle DO
     arrive at the left end `10101` in two different phases depending on `k mod 2`
     (or `k mod 3`, as in `C1=1RH H0=0RC H1=1LI I0=--- I1=1LD`).
     However, chewing `(10)^4` without halting requires using `C1`, `H0`, `H1`
     (and `I1` if routed through `I`) inside the interior `(10)^{2k-3}` loop.
     Consequently, on the opposite parity of k:
       - Either the left-end bounce only uses those already-defined transitions
         and resolves into another `VALID_ODD_K` cascade (for example,
         `C1=1RH H0=0RC H1=1LI I0=--- I1=1LD` reaches `K([1, 22, 0, (3,0)^7, 1^M]; 5)`
         at even k = 20 and `K([24, 0, (3,0)^8, 1^M]; 1)` at odd k = 21),
       - Or it enters an even-B / odd-digit cascade that re-enters `C1` in
         Context 2 (`1001 C[1] 1^{B'}`) on the same defined transitions.
  3. High-Order Right vs Low-Order Left Geometry:
     Because `(0011)^M 001` is on the RIGHT (high-order Ackermann end) while
     `(10)^{2k-3} 11001 C[1]` is on the LEFT (low-order accumulator end), any
     9-state machine that routes a Context-2 transition to `---` (via `I0` or `I1`)
     halts during the evaluation of the first O(1) low-order digits of Round 2,
     while the M_1 digits `(0011)^{M_1 - O(1)}` remain on the right of the tape,
     yielding halting score Σ = B_1 + O(f_ω^{low}) > 3 ↑^20 5.

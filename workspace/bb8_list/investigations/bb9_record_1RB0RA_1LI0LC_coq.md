## 1. What the machine really does (literal checks first, then the Coq statements)
Tokens are D-lengths, D(m) = (10)^m 1. Configurations are written
`Kt ds m r t = 0^inf 11 D(d_n)..D(d_1) D(m) (10)^r 11 (11111100)^t 1001 B> 0^inf`, with:
- ds nearest-first;
- m the a-token;
- t the number of tail blocks. The champion's tail 111001 is t = 0; the rebuild's TAIL is t = 6.

1. **Start-up and champion phase.** As claimed.
   - c0 -->* K(2,0^10;0;0) in 2149 steps.
   - The champion rules then give the first exhaustion K(0^11;0;B) with B = U 10 (U 10 0), i.e. 2B+4 = 2^^13 3.
   - R1-R3 hold on tokens for every tail length t:
     - R1: (m+3, r) -> (m, 2r+4)
     - R2: a = D1, nearest token x+3 -> x, a := 2r+3, r := 2

2. **Rebuild.** It ends earlier than Lambda(b). From K(0^11;0;B) with r = 3B+2 the first family configuration is
   `[4,21,4,20, 1^(4r-19) | a=1]`, r=2, t=6, i.e. digits 0^(12B-11) then D20 D4 D21 D4.
   - Literal: 36r^2+2266r+5002 steps, r = 5..14.
   - Lambda(b) is a later champion-rule snapshot of it.
3. **Champion rules on that list.** The digits right of D20 are ordinary.
   - D20 = "6" (residue 2) is borrowed from exactly like a digit: 20 -> 17 -> ... -> 5 -> 2, with the standard refill.
   - The 12B-12 digits are consumed Ackermann-style.
   - This leads to `[4,21,4,2, 0^k | 0 | b']` with k = 12B-11.
4. **The break: the D2 stage is not R3.**

## 2. The Coq proof (`proofs/BB9_record_1RB0RA.v`)
**Machine-level lemmas.** These were generated by `mexp.py`, a new symbolic executor on top of `autoproof/`:
- **Loops.** When a round peels one block of a run and returns with blocks inserted, the stack is carried as a run of
  its own: `round: X(S v,i) -->* X(v,S i)`, `loop: X(v,i) -->* X(0,i+v)`. Rounds may cross their own stack, which
  autoproof's framing cannot handle.
- Concrete copies in front of the run are absorbed into the loop run.
- Insertions may happen at several places, and an insertion may be split into two stacks growing side by side.
- Inner loops are run in full inside a round.
- Each loop gets a corollary `_z` at the use site.

| lemma | statement (schematic) | size |
|---|---|---|
| R1, R2, R3 | the champion rules on tokens, general tail t, rest of the list `1 >> l` | 113 lemmas |
| REBUILD | `Kt (1^11) 1 (r+5) 0 -->+ Kt (1^(4r+1) ++ [20;4;21;4]) 1 2 6` | 51 stages, 271 lemmas, about 20k literal steps |
| D2STAGE | `Kt (1^(5q+11) ++ [2;4;21;4]) 1 (r+5) 6 -->+ Kt (1^(3q+4) ++ [3q+11;2r+8;2;21;4]) 0 4 8` | 166 lemmas |
| HALTRUN, HALTED | `Kt (1 :: ds) 0 4 t` reaches state I on a 1 | |

**Abstract part** (`abstract.v.in`):
- The family Kt/Kg, with Kg ds suf a b t = Kt (map (3d+1) ds ++ suf) (3a+1) (3b+2) t.
- Wrappers KR1, KR2, KR3, and KR3s (the borrow from a residue-2 token).
- CDP (exact clearing, from BB8_champ35_bound.v), and CLR2: clearing the token 3v+2 behind M+1 zeros gives the counter
  iter v (U (S M)) B.
- init_reach (2149 steps), rebuild, d2_halt.
- Arithmetic:
  - Bx_closed : 2 Bx + 4 = arrow 11 2 (arrow 11 2 4).
  - arrow_pow2, arrow_mod5 : arrow k 2 n mod 5 = 1 for k >= 2, n >= 3.
  - Bx_mod5 : Bx mod 5 = 1, and Bx_ge6.

1. **Uc_le.** U j x <= A (j+2) (2x+4) and X j <= A (j+3) 4, by induction on j.
   - Base: U 0 x = 3x+7, X 0 = 7.

3. **Pv_le, second_le.**

   - The two clearings at depth b+1 add A(b+3)^4, so second b <= A (b+4) (b+6) for every b.

## 5. Pitfalls met
- **lia under busycoq hangs on transparent closed constants.** Helper.v sets
  `zify_convert_to_euclidean_division_equations_flag` and a zify pre-hook. With `Bx := U 10 (U 10 0)` a plain Definition,
  even `Opaque`, `lia` on a goal with `Bx mod 5` ran for tens of minutes. The same lemma took 0.1 s outside busycoq.
  Fix: seal the constant (`Lemma Bx_sig : {b | b = ...}. Qed.  Definition Bx := proj1_sig Bx_sig.`).
- Numerals 0/1 in polymorphic positions (`repeat 1 n`, `Nat.iter b f 1`, `change ... with 1`) parse as symbols under
  Individual92's `sym` scope. Use `1%nat`, or open nat_scope for arithmetic sections.
- `simpl`/`simpl lft` turned `2 * r` into `r + (r + 0)`. Use targeted rewrites (lft_cons, acc3/acc4).
- `follow` only adjusts fully instantiated lemmas (do_adjust does not match a `forall`). Hence the `_z` use-site corollaries.
- A final `finish_norm` cannot prove `[1;0;1;1;0;1;1;0;1;1;0;1]^^r = [1;0;1]^^(4r)`. Targets must use the machine's
  natural block. `endcheck.py` checks all targets in 9 s, before a 45-minute build.

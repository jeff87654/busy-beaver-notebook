5. WHY THE M8-ENGINE SPACE TOPS OUT HERE (structural)
  A certifiable chain needs residue-decided rounds BEFORE any endgame cascade: the machine must leave M8 at E0's far-left
  event (step 8110), as h003 and C7 do.  All other loops enter M8's cascade first.  The cascade's end (rebuild vs halt,
  and c') depends on the endgame class of a tower-sized K.  So those machines are Y-type, the case covered by the peek
  theorem.  Their round counts are not residue-computable.  None of them was seen to halt after more than 2 rounds, so
  they are most likely non-halting.  In the pre-cascade population the best maps give 2 rounds (residue K1 = 0 mod 5
  exits).  h003's 4 rounds remain the maximum.
  Unresolved (not candidates by structure): the 108 deep-run 'ONE' rows that STOP (1 top, then long non-top segments or
  stalls of > 1e9 W2 steps).  Any later top-level rebuild there would start after <= 1 residue round.

WHY IT FAILS
- The only halt is H0.
- The gadget output depends on n, and on 3a+1-n only as a list length, never on residues of a.
- On the gadget's paths, H only ever reads 1 at the real scale.
- An M8-style residue-decided round needs a gadget that tests something like the parity of n' = 3a+1-n, halts in
  one class, and rebuilds cleanly in the other.
- 1LH/1LC has exactly that test (odd n -> halt), but its even-n rebuild leaves junk that kills it at once.

RESIDUES (resid.py; for any follow-up)
- a_f = g(w), g(w) = f^(3w+2)(1) = (5*3^(3w+2)-3)/2.
- g flips parity (g(w) is odd iff w is even), so a_f mod 2 is NOT tower-stable. It depends on the parity of the
  B1-chain length, recursively through the whole run.
- Only 2a_f+3 = 0 mod 3^K is stable.
- A parity-gated design would need a level-table residue engine (U_j mod Q, as in reb7/eng7), not one tower lemma.

SECTION 1: WHY `blank_max_nd < 10` DISCARDED 866 OF THE 919 `has_halt` LEAVES
================================================================================

In `bb8_fw1_redirect`, `noinv_leaves.txt` contained 4,423 modified-engine K-like
leaves without a `Phi_0` or `Phi_1` mod-2 invariant on `invchk.py`'s 24 random
configurations. Of those 4,423 leaves:
  - 3,504 had `has_halt == 0` on `AS = [1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 15, 16]`.
  -   919 had `has_halt == 1` on `AS`.

To filter the 919 `has_halt == 1` leaves, `bb8_fw1_redirect` ran `kblank.exe` for
400,000 steps from the blank tape and required `blank_max_nd >= 10` (at least 10
digits in a K-configuration within 400,000 steps). Only 53 leaves passed (`cls53.txt`),
discarding 866 leaves!

Why did 866 leaves have `blank_max_nd < 10` at 400,000 steps?
1. In `F3` bases `[d, f, o]`, the first blank-tape exhaustion occurs at `A_1 = 1`
   (`E_1(1)`), which rebuilds a short list of **1 to 9 digits** (`blank_max_nd` in
   `1..9`). Evaluating that 2-to-9 digit list via Ackermann recursion (`B_1, B_2, ...`)
   takes `>> 400,000` steps (typically `10^8` to `f_9` steps!), so at step 400,000
   the machine is still inside Round 1 with `blank_max_nd <= 9`, and only builds its
   massive list (`10^42` to `f_9` digits) at `E_2(A_2)`!
2. Similarly, in `F2 / F3` bases `[a, b, c, e, g, i, j, n, p, q, t]`, any leaf whose
   Round-1 list has length `1..9` and takes `> 400,000` steps to clear was blindly
   discarded by `blank_max_nd < 10`.

To audit all 866 discarded leaves without blind spots, we compiled
`kblank_detail.exe` (`small_nd_audit/kblank_detail.cpp`) and probed all 919 leaves
with `kmap2.exe` (`probe_919.tsv`) on `D(1..12)`, `B_1..B_6`, `LB_1..LB_3`, and
`E(1..16)`.

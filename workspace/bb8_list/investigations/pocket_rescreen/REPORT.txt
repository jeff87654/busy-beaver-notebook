  3e8 steps. Each uses a distinct transition set. Backref (depth 40) decides none.
  famT14 found the T1 run coding in only 7 rows. In the rest the counter lives in ZERO GAPS: the first exhaustion turns
  the accumulator into a gap of ~2b zeros, and that gap is then worked off in steps of +-1 to -4 per doubling of B.
  That is exponential per round, not f_omega. Hand-read on #60 by famT14 (HANDOFF in famT14/notes.txt).
  Cheap size filter (tools/gapsnap.exe): snapshots of the zero-gap and 1-run structure at 1e7/3e7/1e8/3e8 steps
  (gapsnap_r1lg.txt -> gapcls_r1lg.tsv -> r1lg_final.tsv).
    UNARY-GAP 351: one big gap, nearly constant (e.g. 96, 93, 90, 87) while the largest 1-run grows (#60 shape).
    MULTI-GAP const 126: an inert big gap plus a second gap shrinking by 3 per checkpoint. Same unary consumption.
    GAP-GROWS 65: the rebuild is seen inside the window. A gap of size ~ the accumulator appears (12228 -> 12273).
    MULTI-GAP changing 40: repeated fixed gaps (218) whose count grows linearly; linear span (1.65e6 at 3e8).
    -> 582 NOT-RECORD-CLASS by this filter. A further 7 show a gap by 2e9 and 7 halt within 2e9 steps (small).
  UNCLASSIFIED 523 (r1lg_final.tsv, label UNCLASSIFIED). These show no gap structure up to 3e8 steps, or up to 2e9
  for the 71 of them run that far. This literal filter cannot tell a binary/unary counter from a T1-type digit list:
  both show a few long 1-runs and gaps <= 2, and spans of ~16000-33000 at 3e8.

  The T1-coded relatives were all rebuild-forever, and the gap-coded ones are exponential per round. A record would

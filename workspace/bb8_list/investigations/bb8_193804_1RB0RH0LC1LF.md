`xs^^n *> l <{{q}} c >> r -->* l <{{q}} c >> ys^^n *> r` (and the mirror for `>`): simulate one copy xs together with the
behind window c, require the head to exit in the same state, the first |c| cells of the result to equal c again, and
ys = the remaining cells. Proved in Coq by the usual `induction n; execute; follow IHn; finish_norm`. The lemmas are derived on
the fly by a planner8 matcher (`pipe_matcher`) at each point where the head sits on a run.

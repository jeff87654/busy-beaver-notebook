# BB(8) holdout, subtask 76450: `1RB0LC_0RC0RF_0RD0RH_1LE---_0LF1LG_1RD1LA_1RC1LA_0LF0LA`

Source: holdout of BB(8) subtask 76450 (mxdys enumerator @ a41db6c). The mxdys NG/RWL/CPS pre-filter could not
decide it, and no already-proved machine is within 10 transitions of it.
- Pipeline growth at 2 / 10 / 40 CPU-s was 7,192 / 18,843 / 33,228 digits at 1,769 / 2,583 / 3,588 loops.
- That is 4.07 -> 7.30 -> 9.26 digits per loop; the run stopped at the time limit.
- The undefined (halting) transition is D1.

## Verdict

**The machine never halts.**
- It is a two-level tripling counter with a reset.
  - An outer counter of (10) pairs pays for one tripling of an inner counter per pair.
  - When the outer counter is empty, the inner counter (as a block of 1s) becomes the next outer counter, about 9 times larger.
- Its size therefore grows like a tower of 3s: x -> 3^(9x+8).
- It has no sigma, so it is **not a BB(8) record candidate** and is irrelevant to the 2↑¹¹2↑¹¹3 bar.

**Confidence: proved (Coq PASS).**
- `bb8_list/proofs/BB8_76450_1RB0LC.v` proves `Theorem nonhalt : ~ halts tm c0.` (busycoq, Individual82,
  Coq 8.20.1, about 18 s to compile).
- `python check.py BB8_76450_1RB0LC.v` printed (`bb8_076450_1RB0LC0RC0RF/check_py.out`):
  `PASS BB8_76450_1RB0LC.v: 1RB0LC_0RC0RF_0RD0RH_1LE---_0LF1LG_1RD1LA_1RC1LA_0LF0LA never halts (machine-checked, no axioms)`.
- Every rule was also checked by exact literal simulation: 1,228 cases, 0 failures (`check_rules.py`).
- A literal run of 3·10^9 steps from the blank tape found 13 consecutive family configurations. Each one was the
  predicted successor of the one before (0 mismatches), and the machine did not halt (`trajectory.js`).

## Configuration family

Every configuration of interest has the head in state D on the blank right edge, two blank cells behind it:

```
0^inf W 0 0 D> 0^inf
Cc(a, k) : W = 1 (10)^a 11 (10)^k 1
Dc(a, i) : W = 1 (10)^a 1^(6i+3)          (= Cc(a, 0) after the inner pairs are converted into 6i more 1s)
```

- a is the outer counter and k the inner counter.
- Dc is the family used in the Coq proof.

  - a left sweep over 1-blocks (a hole moves left 3 cells per 7 steps, `cr_GL_*`);
  - a right sweep in state F that carries a 0 hole through the 1s (`swF3`, `swF6`);
  - a right sweep in C that rewrites 111111 as 010101 (`cr_CR_111111_010101`, used by R0o, T1, T2).

The literal checks cover the parameter ranges in `check_rules.py`. For rules with 0^inf on the left, the target is
matched at the head, as in the Coq statements, because the word may shift left.

**Why D1 is never reached.**
- The only halting situation is R1's sweep arriving at X 0 1^(3m+2) where X ends in 1.
  - It halts in all 40 checked cases, which are listed in `check_rules.py`.
  - With 0^inf on the left, the same residue is harmless (T2).
- The family never produces that situation:
  - Inner rounds start at 1^1 and add 6, so they stay 1 (mod 3).
  - Each round ends with 1^(6i+3) behind a (10) pair (R0o).
  - At the top, the word is 1^(6i+4), then 1^(6i+9), then 1^(18i+32) = 1^(6j+2), and that last one has only blank
    tape on its left.
- The Coq proof does not need this argument: it shows that every Dc(a, i) has a Dc successor.

## Pipeline growth data explained

At 2,000 loops (10 s), `Quick_Sim.py --recursive` with block size 6 shows this tape (`qs2000_excerpt.txt`):

```
0^inf 110101 010101^(~10^^2.3) 011101 010101 111111^(~10^^2.6) (111110) F> 111110 0^inf
```

- Read as a word, this is 1 (10)^a 11 (10)^4 1^n.
  - a ≈ 10^121 is the second outer counter.
  - n has about 10^4 digits.
- So the machine is inside the second cycle, and the head is in the middle of an R1 sweep.
- Each outer pair multiplies the inner counter by 3, which adds 0.477 digits.
- At 9.26 digits per loop, Quick_Sim's rules therefore consume roughly 20 outer pairs per loop.
  - That is more than the 10 per loop that a single tripling rule capped at `--max-num-reps` 10 would allow.
  - The likely reason is the recursive prover's fused level-2 rules (it reports 2 of them), which multiply by
    powers of 3 per application.
- The rising digits per loop is this fusion, not a sign of halting or of super-tower growth.

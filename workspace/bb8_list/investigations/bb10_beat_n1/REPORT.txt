  - In every non-halting `E0` call (`S0`, `P1`, `P2`, `P4`, `E`), when state `E` reads `0`

        Total steps: `k + 3`. Resulting tape: `... <C 0_{p-k-2} 0_{p-k-1} 0^k 1_p`.

2.2 Why `---0LJ_0LE0LJ` Writes +1 Extra `1` at `HALT`
-----------------------------------------------------
At the final `HALT` phase (`FarE(k) ++ D(0) ++ D(1)^w ++ D(0) ++ D(f)`), after `w` steps of
`R2` reduce `w` to `0`, the head enters `FarE(k)`, which contains the 5-zero run `00000`:
  `1011010101010101001 00000 10111100100100100100`
When `E0` is called inside `FarE(k)`, the tape to the left of `p` has `k = 1`, preceded by
`000` instead of `10`:
  `... 0_{p-4} 0_{p-3} 0_{p-2} 1_{p-1} [0]_p`
  * In `n1` (`0LJ0LI_---0LC`):
    - `E0(1LI)` writes `1` at `p`, moves left to `p-1` (`1`) in `I`.
    - `I1(0LI)` erases `1` at `p-1`, moves left to `p-2` (`0`) in `I`.
    - `I0(0LJ)` writes `0` at `p-2`, moves left to `p-3` (`0`) in `J`.
    - `J0 = ---` HALTS at `p-3` (`000 0 <J 0 0 1 ...`).
  * In `n1_plus1` (`---0LJ_0LE0LJ`):
    - `E0(1LI)` writes `1` at `p`, moves left to `p-1` (`1`) in `I`.
    - `I1(0LJ)` erases `1` at `p-1`, moves left to `p-2` (`0`) in `J`.
    - `J0(0LE)` writes `0` at `p-2`, moves left to `p-3` (`0`) in `E`.
    - `E0(1LI)` WRITES AN EXTRA `1` at `p-3` and moves left to `p-4` (`0`) in `I`!
    - `I0 = ---` HALTS at `p-4` (`000 <I 1 0 0 1 ...`)!

Thus `n1_plus1` halts 1 step later with 1 additional `1` on the tape (`2*w + f + 3*k + 23`
vs `2*w + f + 3*k + 22`).

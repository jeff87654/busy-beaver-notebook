OPEN CANDIDATES: potentially record-beating machines we can't settle yet
=========================================================================
Purpose: a durable list of machines that MIGHT beat a current record but that we couldn't decide (halting, score, or
whether they beat the bar), so we can come back with better tools or ideas. C04 is the precedent: it was set aside as
"Collatz-hard" and later settled by a parity invariant.

How to add an entry (agents: append with a shell heredoc; keep the format; newest at the bottom of its section):
  ID / machine string / states
  bar it might beat, and the estimated level if it halts
  status: OPEN, or what is known (evidence vs proof)
  the obstacle: exactly what is undecided, and what would settle it
  data / folder / who found it / date
Move an entry to SETTLED (with the outcome) instead of deleting it.

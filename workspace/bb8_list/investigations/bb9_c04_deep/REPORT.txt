3. VERDICT, STATUS, WHAT IS NOT COMPUTABLE
------------------------------------------
PROVED (paper/computation): parity invariance and the key set, given the sub-step rule; residues of B; exhaustion #2's path.
STRONG EVIDENCE (literal simulation in representative worlds, hundreds of contexts, no counterexample): the sub-step rule for
L >= 60 in the cascade context; the endgame table; the post-ESC counter modes and their halts; size-independence (R >> P or
R << P give the same forms; the rules are local).
HEURISTIC: which fine class occurs.  With a uniform prior on an odd start: key 31: 0.52, 49: 0.35, 37: 0.08, 55: 0.03,
43: 0.02; P' mod 5 uniform =>  P(HALT 3-4 clears after ESC) ~ 0.58, P(long counter mode first) ~ 0.42.  The real class is
decided by the full base-5 expansion of B = (2^^13 3 - 4)/2 (the orbit's entry point) and by the number j of cascade
exhaustions (through P' mod 5): not computable in practice.  So C04 is a machine that provably (modulo the model) halts
while its exact score is beyond computation; only bounds are available.
NOT a reduction to an open problem: nothing is open; the uncertainty is purely computational.

4. SCORE BOUND
--------------
At the halt the tape holds all parked tokens D(6R_i+9), the far tokens, the consumed parked list and the last counter-mode
token T = 6R_last+7, so sigma > 6 R_last.  Chain of lower bounds:
  * P'' (the depth of the ESC clear) >= 12R2 - 10 - 7j - 3j'' - 60 with j + j'' <= log_{5/3}(36B+73)+1 (each exhaustion
    shrinks L by at least 3/5), so P'' > 11 R2 (R2 is a tower far above B).
  * R_ESC = U_{P''}(U_{P''}(0))  (digit 2 at depth P'', b = 0), 2R_ESC+4 = 2^^(P''+1)(2^^(P''+1) 4) > 2^^(P''+1) 4.
  * Every later clear is of a digit of value 2R+2 >= 2R_ESC+2 at depth >= P''-1: R_last > R_ESC.
  Hence sigma > 2^^(11 R2) 4 with 2R2+4 > 2^^(12B+25) 4, i.e. sigma > 2^^(2^^(12B+25) 4) 4.
  FGH (record's inequalities f_omega(n) >= 2^^(n-1) n, f_omega(n) <= 2^^(n+1) 4):  sigma > f_omega(11R2-1) and
  11R2-1 > f_omega(12B+24), so sigma > f_omega(f_omega(12B+24)).  fw2's bound is f_omega(f_omega(465009)) and
  12B+24 > 2^^12 3 >> 465009.  Upper bound (not needed): sigma < f_omega(13 R2) roughly (at most ~0.6P'+2 clears of depth <= P'').

6. WHAT A COQ PROOF WOULD REQUIRE
---------------------------------
(a) Level 0: the record's development adapted to C04's table (same K-rules; the step-level lemmas must be regenerated for
    the new gadget).  (b) Exhaustion #1 rebuild, parametric in b (like the record's 51-stage rebuild lemma).  (c) The
    champion clearing with the U_j functions (reusable).  (d) THE SUB-STEP LEMMA: parametric in L (>= 60) and in the
    parked-region contents (the sub-step sweeps the parked list, so the lemma is parametric in P as well), 4 residue cases
    + the stop case + the accumulator-to-token/list conversions (6R+9, 6R+12, 12R-10) + the 3 tail blocks; plus the
    top-level first step for r = 4.  (e) The cascade: a well-founded induction on L over the configuration family of 2.5 with
    the invariant "L odd, L = 1 mod 3", down to the key.  (f) The endgame: five keys x (for key 31) five P' residues, each a
    short sequence of parametric lemmas (the ESC sweep over 1^P'' is parametric in P''; counter mode = induction on C; the
    nested cycle once).  (g) Residue facts: B mod 5^5 (for exh#2's branches) and parity of 36B+73.  (h) The score bound.
Size: the sub-step lemma family is the hard part (each sub-step is a sweep with several phases visible in tmsim raw traces:
(110)^a / (10011)^b / (100)^c encodings).  Estimate 3-6x the record's effort; feasible with the symbolic-executor workflow
(generate lemma statements from pkf4/tmsim traces, check step by step), but a multi-day project.  Not started (per rules).

    with $c = 26$, or $+10$ collapses in `1RB0RG_1RC1RB_0RD0LH_0LE0LD_0LF1LE_1RA1LF_0LD0RB_1LJ1LI_1RB1LH_---1LD`
    with $c = 14$).
  * Pre-Collapse Mod-4 Splitting (`c = 0` `E_3` halters):
    Among all $15 \times 160 = 2,400$ extensions of the 15 `c = 0` `E_3` halters, 4 machines split odd $k$
    (`P = 2`) into $k \equiv 1 \pmod 4 \to \text{K}$ ($dL = 2, I = 0$) and $k \equiv 3 \pmod 4 \to \text{H}^*$ ($c = 18$):
      `1RB0RG_1RC1RB_0RD1RH_0LE0LD_0LF1LE_1RA1LF_0LD0RB_1RD0RI_0LG0RJ_---1RG`
      `1RB0RG_1RC1RB_0RD1RH_0LE0LD_0LF1LE_1RA1LF_0LD0RB_1LE0RI_0LG0RJ_---1RG`
      `1RB0RG_1RC1RB_0RD1RH_0LE0LD_0LF1LE_1RA1LF_0LD0RB_1RD0RI_1LH0RJ_---1RG`
      `1RB0RG_1RC1RB_0RD1RH_0LE0LD_0LF1LE_1RA1LF_1LC0RB_1RD0RI_1LH0RJ_---1RG`
    By the Leftmost-Borrow Law ($A_r \equiv I_r = 1 \implies A_r \equiv 1 \pmod 4$; $I_r = 0 \implies A_r \equiv 2 \pmod 4$),
    Round 1 ($k_1 = 20 \equiv 0 \pmod 4, I_1 = 1$) maps to Round 2 ($k_2 \equiv 2 \pmod 4, I_2 = 0$),
    which maps to Round 3 ($k_3 \equiv 3 \pmod 4, I_3 = 1$). Thus these 4 machines still halt at the
    end of Round 3 (`R = 3`), while adding $c = 18$ sequential intra-gadget Ackermann collapses of
    height $s_1 = k_3 - 2 \approx 3 A_2 \approx f_\omega^2(20)$ at the end of Round 3 (raising their
    halting value from $\sim f_\omega^3(20)$ to $\sim f_\omega^4(20)$).

1. WHERE A HALT WOULD HAVE TO HAPPEN (cell level)
-------------------------------------------------
H1: F is entered only by E1 = 0RF.  E entered by a left move (C1, D1 = 1LE) reads the 1 just written and F reads a 1.
    D0 = 1RE puts E on the 0 that C0 just wrote.  So a halt needs A1 = 1RE (A reading 1) followed by cells "1 0":
    the A/B right sweep must hit "1 1 0".  Literal blank run, 1e9 steps: A1 occurs 97 times (all G1->A1, at the left
    tape end or during the list build), always followed by E0 (A1E1 = 0 times).
H2: G is entered only by D1 = 0RG; a halt needs D1 followed by a 0.  1e9 steps: D1->G 1.67e8 times, always G1.
c01/c02: A is entered only by B1 = 0RA; a halt needs the B/A right sweep over (10)* (B1 A0 B1 A0 ...) to meet "11".
    1e9 steps: B1->A 2.45e8 times, always A0.
The four bases sit on the bbchallenge holdout list, so they ran 1e9+ steps there without halting as well.

-----------------------------------------------

    (1,388 runs, ctlscan.tsv).  The spurious halt is always the sweep in section 1 running into a blank-looking window.
(b) Rule-level structure (from nonlist/hand/groupA.txt, groupC.txt, re-checked here):
    the tape is ACC | G1 | ... | Gn (groups 10^x 01^y split by 00 at K=2).  Every group drains through (1,0) as its
    last nonzero value, and an emptied group is just zeros, so the far end is blank beyond the top nonempty group.
    Hence EVERY list exhaustion, in every round, is the configuration "ACC | (1,0)" + blank, with the ACC in one of
    its cycle phases.  c01's natural start is the same configuration (top counter 01^9 counts down to 01^1, then the
    first list is built), so c01 meets its exhaustion form already at 1.27e7 steps.
(c) Exhaustion sweeps (exact K=2 macro simulation with run acceleration, src/xs.cpp; each run continues through the
    exhaustion, the complete rebuild and 0.1-2.5M further macro steps into the new list):
      H1  E|10^2 01^b 00 10,  E|11 01^b 00 10,  C|10 01^b 00 10   (the three phases of the last L1 cycle; dumps at
          steps 6847/7497/7798 of the natural run), b = 1..200 (+ 201..229/232 at 2.5M macro): 750 runs, 0 halts.
      H2  same three phases (D/B for E/C), b = 1..264: 792 runs, 0 halts.

      c02 same template: 2,640 (q <= 120) runs, 0 halts.
    The cycle maps every orbit through a small-p (c01) / start-of-cycle (H1) point, so these templates cover every
    ACC phase.
(d) The rebuild is exactly uniform in the accumulator: for H1 and H2 the rebuilt list after "ACC|(1,0)" with ACC

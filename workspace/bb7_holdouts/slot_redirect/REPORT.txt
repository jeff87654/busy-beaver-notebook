4. WHY IT FAILS
  The M8 mechanism needs an Ackermann digit-list engine whose exhaustion reaches an undefined slot.
  Rows with 12 or fewer defined transitions do not seem to support that in the list. Every graded row with a halting
  or rebuilding exhaustion of record interest (CHAMPION, R4-SOME, HALT?, HALT-SOME, CHECK) has exactly one '---'.
  The c231ext result says an 8th state is not enough on champ_231. Together with this one, spare-slot redirects inside
  BB(7) are closed for the digit-list class.
  NOT covered:
    - pf7-FAIL rows outside the 1% sample and the first-pass hits. pf7 is calibrated to zero misses, but that is
      empirical.
    - Engines that neither dlscan2 nor the nonlist detector sees.
    - Unused DEFINED transitions in 1-'---' rows (free-transition variants). That is a different construction.

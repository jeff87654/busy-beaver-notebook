Detailed Blank-Tape Verification of the b=4 Family (#1-#5):
  On the blank tape at K=2, machines #1-#5 build a static alternating list `(10 11)xN` (or `(11 10)xN`, N = 15 or 34) in Stage 1
  (finishing before 10,000 base steps). In Stage 2, the tape has the exact form:
      <far-end> (10 11)xV  [binary word in {10, 11}^(2(N-V))]  11^A 01^B <head>
  Every sweep of the right-hand doubler `11^A 01^B` doubles `A + B` and increments the binary word in `{10, 11}` by +1.
  When the binary word reaches all-ones (`11^(2(N-V))`), the carry propagates into `(10 11)xV`, consuming one `(10 11)` item
  (`V -> V - 1`) and extending the binary word by 2 bits (`11^2 10^(2(N-V)+1)`). Thus the k-th consumed item requires
  exact 4^k + 1 forced doubler rounds (3, 17, 65, 257, 1025, 4097, 16385). When all N items are absorbed and the 2N-bit binary
  word overflows into the far-end marker, the machine hits its halt transition at ~2^(4^N) < 2↑↑5 steps (rank7 level < 5).
